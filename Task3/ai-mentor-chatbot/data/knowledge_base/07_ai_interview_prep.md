# AI/ML Interview Preparation Guide

## What Interviewers Actually Look For
Beyond model accuracy, interviewers assess whether you can explain design decisions: why you chose a specific architecture, how you handled data issues, and what tradeoffs you made under constraints like limited compute or time.

## Core Questions to Be Ready For
- Explain the bias-variance tradeoff in your own words
- Walk through how you would deploy a model to production
- What would you do if your model performed well in training but poorly in production
- Explain the difference between precision and recall, and when each matters more
- How does a transformer's attention mechanism work at a high level

## Project Walkthrough Structure
When explaining a project, follow this order: problem statement, why you chose this approach over alternatives, key technical challenges and how you solved them, results, and what you would improve with more time. This structure signals maturity beyond just "it works."

## Red Flags to Avoid
- Not knowing why you chose a specific library or model over alternatives
- Being unable to explain what happens when input is invalid or edge cases occur
- Overstating results without mentioning limitations
- Copy-pasted code you cannot explain line by line

## System Design for AI Roles
Be ready to sketch how you would design a system end-to-end: data ingestion, preprocessing, model serving, monitoring, and retraining triggers. This matters more for AI Engineer and AI Platform Engineer roles than deep model internals.
